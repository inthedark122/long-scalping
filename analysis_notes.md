Incorrect interpretation: "NTPS dropping = low conviction = likely fakeout at resistance."
Correct interpretation: "NTPS low + price drifting up = sustained retail accumulation — expect continuation until BSG climax."
Application: Do NOT add counter-trend entries during low-NTPS directional moves. Wait for BSG > 2.0 spike to confirm exhaustion before shorting aggressively.
