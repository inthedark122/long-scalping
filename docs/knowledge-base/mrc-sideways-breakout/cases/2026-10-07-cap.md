## What to verify in later cases

- Whether price stayed within both MRC outer lines for a measurable duration
  before the break.
- Which MRC line first broke and what confirmation preceded the expansion.
- Net sideways PnL after fees before the stop.
- Whether the sharp dump was followed by sideways movement or a slow recovery,
  and how the MRC mean slope changed through that transition.
